Budget

A personal iPhone app that tracks spending against monthly budgets, using the transaction emails Discovery Bank sends to your Gmail.

What it does

  • Signs in to Gmail with read-only access and finds only Discovery Bank's “Transaction update” emails.
  • Reads the amount, shop, date and account from each email and turns it into a transaction.
  • Sorts transactions into categories such as groceries, fuel and eating out, and learns from the categories you pick.
  • Tracks each category against a monthly budget and can notify you at 80% and 100% of a budget.

Your data

Everything the app reads stays on your iPhone. There are no servers, accounts, adverts or analytics. The app never sends, changes or deletes email. Read the privacy policy.

Budget is a personal project and isn't made by or connected to Discovery Bank or Google.
