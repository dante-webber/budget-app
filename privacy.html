Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

What the app accesses

Budget asks Google for one permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It uses that permission only to search for and read Discovery Bank transaction alert emails, which match the search shown in the app's settings. It doesn't read other email, and it can't send, change or delete email.

What it keeps, and where

  • From each matching email: the amount, merchant or reference, date, account and card endings, available balance, and the email's subject and text. These are kept in the app's database on your iPhone.
  • Your Gmail address and Google sign-in token are kept in your iPhone's keychain.
  • Your budgets, categories and settings are kept on your iPhone.

Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The app has no servers, accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking. It only connects to Google, to sign in and to read the emails above.

Google API data

Budget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you your own transactions and budgets. It isn't transferred, sold, used for advertising, or read by people.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Gmail in the app's Settings to revoke its access. You can also remove “Budget” at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date above changes with it.
